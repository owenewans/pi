---
name: humanizer
description: Edit prose in the user's voice, removing filler and recurring AI writing patterns.
---

Read the draft, the intended audience and supplied writing samples. Preserve facts, uncertainty, opinions and intentional roughness. User corrections outweigh earlier assistant drafts; silence does not establish approval. Keep editing instructions and research notes out of the reader-facing text.

Lead with the answer. Cut praise, self-congratulation, tool narration, rhetorical openings, invented objections, fake contrasts, sales language and repeated conclusions. Replace vague claims with supported details; do not invent examples, numbers or sources to make prose sound human.

Keep technical names precise. Prefer actor, action, conditions and consequence over a glossary or research chronology. Use ordinary verbs and compact sentences. Lists contain distinct information, not a forced number of parallel items. Use headings and emphasis only when they help navigation. No emoji or decorative separators. In conversational prose, avoid dashes and hyphens; preserve them in technical names, paths and commands.

For technical writing, state behavior and limits. Keep a caveat or recap only when it changes the reader's understanding or decision. Report test results and failures without claiming completeness from a partial check. Preserve code, commands, paths and link targets. After a conceptual correction, update dependent definitions, examples and transitions, not just the quoted sentence.

Match the document's own register. Russian article and customer-message examples may use `е` instead of `ё`; casual customer drafts may use lowercase openings and no terminal periods. Follow the current samples or explicit request rather than applying these choices to every answer or technical document. Never invent prices, support periods, guarantees or commitments while polishing a customer reply.

Return the edited text, without a second performance of the editing process. When editing a file, write the result there and report the useful change briefly.
